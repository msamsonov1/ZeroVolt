01

Springer-Zawodzinski Proton Transport

Membrane proton conductivity $\sigma_m$ in perfluorosulfonic acid (PFSA / Nafion) polymers is governed by the water content parameter $\lambda = \frac{n(\text{H}_2\text{O})}{n(\text{SO}_3^-)}$. Below $\lambda \approx 4$, percolation paths collapse, creating an exponential surge in ohmic cell resistance $R$:

$$\sigma_m(T, \lambda) = (0.005139\lambda - 0.00326) \cdot \exp\left[ 1268 \left( \frac{1}{303} - \frac{1}{T} \right) \right] \quad [\text{S/cm}]$$

The high-frequency intercept on the Nyquist plot ($f \sim 10\,\text{kHz}$) isolates the purely real ohmic resistance $R_{\text{HFR}} = t_m / \sigma_m$, where $t_m$ is the active membrane thickness, decoupling ionic hydration from slower charge-transfer kinetics.

Key Threshold: Critical Dry-Out occurs when $\lambda < 3.2 \implies R_{\text{HFR}} > 280\text{ m}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}^2$.
02

Two-Phase Darcy Permeability & Droplet Drag

In closed-loop lunar RFC stacks without gravitational draining ($g \approx 1.62\,\text{m/s}^2$), product water liquid condensation clogs Gas Diffusion Layer (GDL) pores. The modified Darcy-Ergun pressure gradient exhibits steep non-linear pressure jumps as liquid saturation $s$ increases:

$$\Delta P = \frac{\mu_g \cdot L}{K_0 (1 - s)^3} \cdot v_g + \beta \rho_g v_g^2 + \sum_{k} \frac{2 \gamma \cos(\theta_c)}{r_{\text{pore}, k}}$$

Where $s$ is liquid saturation, $K_0$ is baseline permeability, and $\gamma \cos\theta_c$ quantifies the capillary barrier pressure. Liquid slug condensation drives $\Delta P$ upward even while the bulk flow velocity $v_g$ remains steady.

Key Threshold: Flooding onset verified when $d(\Delta P)/dt > 12\,\text{kPa/min}$ with nominal $R_{\text{HFR}}$.
03

Observer State Inversion $(R, P)$

The fundamental breakthrough of ZeroVolt is decoupling state ambiguities using a vector observer in $(R, P)$ phase-space. A single state metric (e.g., cell voltage or total pressure) confuses mass-transfer degradation with membrane degradation:

$$\Phi(R, P) = \begin{bmatrix} \alpha_1 (R - R_{\text{nom}}) \\ \alpha_2 (P - P_{\text{nom}}) \end{bmatrix}, \quad \nabla \times \mathbf{J}_{\text{phase}} \ne 0$$

Quadrant trajectory mapping instantaneously resolves the exact failure mode:
• Quadrant I ($+R, -P$): Membrane Dry-Out (Micro-crack hazard)
• Quadrant II ($R_{\text{nom}}, +P$): Cathode Liquid Flooding (Reactant starvation)

Response latency: < 250 milliseconds from sensor ADC to valve command.
